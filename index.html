03 / Work

Art from the Heart Initiative

The job

A student club at Little Rock Central High School makes free craft kits that teach kids about their health. Families, partners and new members needed one place that felt as warm as the kits do.

What I built

A custom hand-coded site with its own look: follow-along video tutorials that only load when you press play, swipeable photo albums for the club's events, plus leader, partner, research, contact, privacy and accessibility pages.

My role

I'm the club's Website Manager, so I built the site and I still keep it up to date. A November 2026 refresh kept the look and every page, cut the events page from about 2.1 MB to 0.8 MB, and stopped pages from shifting as they load.

06 / Questions

Questions, answered.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?
Any kind. Restaurants, contractors, shops, salons, nonprofits, startups, personal brands. If you need a clean, fast website, I can build it.
How much does a website cost?
One-page sites start at $450, and a website plus Google profile starts at $600. See Prices. Send me a few details and you'll get a flat price in writing within 24 hours. No hidden fees.
How long does it take?
A one-page site takes about a week once I have your content. Bigger sites take about 1 to 2 weeks. You'll get a firm timeline with your quote.
Can you get me to the top of Google?
Nobody can honestly promise a ranking. What I can do is build the site the way Google asks for: fast, clear, and easy to read. I can also set up your Google profile so your hours, address and phone number match everywhere.
I don't have photos or text ready. Is that okay?
Yes. Send what you have and I'll help shape the rest. I can write clear page copy with you and work with your existing photos, logo, or social media.
Can you add booking, forms, or online ordering?
Yes. I connect the tools you already use, like booking systems, contact forms, menus and payment links, or help you pick the right one.
Do I own the website?
Yes, always. The site and the domain are yours. If you ever want to move it or manage it yourself, you can.
How do I update it after launch?
Text or email me what you need. With a Care Plan, small changes are included each month. Without one, I quote the update up front and turn it around fast, so you never have to learn a website builder.
