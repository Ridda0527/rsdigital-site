Questions? Answers.

Does it work with my booking system?
Yes. I connect your site to whatever booking tool you already use — Square Appointments, Vagaro, Booksy, GlossGenius, Acuity, Calendly, and more. Your Book Now buttons drop clients straight into your real booking page. Don't have one yet? I'll help you pick and set one up.
Do I own the website?
Yes, always. The site and the domain are yours. If you ever want to take it somewhere else or manage it yourself, you can, with zero friction.
How long does it take to build?
Most one-page booking sites go live within 3–7 days once I have your photos and service menu. Full salon and studio sites take about 1–2 weeks. You'll get a firm timeline in the proposal before we start.
How do I update the site after launch?
I handle all your edits. New prices, new photos, new hours, a seasonal promo — just text or email me and I turn it around fast. It's all covered under the monthly care plan.
How much is hosting?
A one-page site can be hosted free on a fast platform like Vercel. A custom domain (like yoursalon.com) runs about $15–$20 a year, paid directly to the registrar — not billed through me. On the monthly care plan, hosting is included.
Are there contracts?
One-time builds are a simple project agreement. The monthly care plan is month-to-month — cancel whenever, no lock-in.
What if I'm not happy with the build?
I charge 50% to start and 50% on launch, and I don't take final payment until you're happy. One full round of revisions is included, and I'll keep refining details beyond that until we get it right.
